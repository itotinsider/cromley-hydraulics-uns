  • Plant overview → Overview; the cross-section → Dam section (🎓 Dam view)

  • Governor & HPU → Powerhouse
  • Alarms & events, Trends → Alarms & events, Trends

Old links with a display address (for example /scada-classic#unit/Unit1) open the new display.
