Level 0–1 · Process, machines & training

Training simulator · not part of the plant ↗

Instructor Station

Starts hidden fault scenarios A–H (bearing degradation, strainer blockage, rough zone, HPU wear, shear pin, unbalance, RTD drift, low head) and more failure modes, and shows the ground truth. Nothing here is published: the faults must be found in the data.
Simulators · the "real" plant

dam-sim and the CH turbine PLCs

dam-sim: reservoir, spillway, bottom outlet, three units with governor and plant controller; publishes the customer tree. ch-plc-sim: one CH PLC per turbine (thermal, HPU, vibration models); publishes the CH tree at ~18 KB/s per turbine (1 s values + raw waveforms). A few dual-element sensors are also hardwired to the customer's unit controller.
