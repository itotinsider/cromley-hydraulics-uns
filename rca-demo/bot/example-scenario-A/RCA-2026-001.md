## 1 Problem statement

On 3 November 2026 at 09:00, the CH monitoring PLC raised a high-high alarm (85.0 °C) on Unit 1 turbine guide bearing (TGB) pad 3. The shift reduced load to 34 MW at 09:14 and stopped the unit in a controlled way at 09:48–09:51.

## 6 Hypotheses and verification (logic tree)

Symptom: one TGB pad (pad 3) rising over about 16 days to 85 °C. Pad 1 (both elements) and pads 2 and 4 stayed below 75 °C. Every FMECA failure mode that could produce this symptom is listed.

On 10-13 and 11-02 the load (37.2 MW) and cooling-water inlet (14.0 °C) were the same. Between those days pad 3 rose +7.8 K, pads 1B, 2 and 4 rose +1.2, +2.0 and +2.0 K, and the TGB oil rose +0.8 K. Over the same period U2 and U3 TGB pad 3 offsets were constant (+2.09 K and +2.29 K every day) and their oil was unchanged. Pad-to-pad spread on U1 passed CH's "investigate above 5 K" (CH-DOC-OM-0101 §4.3) on 11-01/11-02, with no alarm for it.

**E2 – Measurement cross-checks.** Pad 1 element B minus element A was −0.4 to −0.2 K for the whole period (agreement within 2 K, OP-07 §3.3). The largest 1-s step between 08:00 and 11:30 on 11-03 was 0.30 K on pad 3 and 0.27–0.33 K on the other pads, so there were no spikes. Pad 3 crossed 75 °C slowly, from 74.59 to 74.96 °C between 23:48 and 23:57 (1-min). Sources: `work/a3_u1_1s.py`, `work/a4_context_checks.py`, `CH-FR-1987-0042_1s.csv.gz`.

**E3 – Standstill test.** The pad 3 offset follows heat input. At 34 MW the excess stayed at +12.5 to +12.95 K over pads 2/4 (09:15–09:45). After the stop it fell to +1.63 K at 09:55 and −0.08 K at 10:00, then stayed between −0.3 and −0.5 K up to 11:30 (pad 3 45.4 °C, pad 2 45.0 °C at 11:30). A series resistance in the RTD loop (the CH-SB-2026-03 mechanism) adds a constant temperature offset whether the unit runs or not, as in RCA-2024-011 ("fault persisted with the unit stopped"). That is not what happened here.

**E4 – Cooling, oil level, operating point, vibration (U1, 10-13 → 11-03, while generating)** (`work/a4_context_checks.py`). TB flow 8.0 L/s (constant), GB 11.9–12.1, air coolers 59.8–60.2, owner total 79.8–80.2 L/s. TGB oil level 55.6–56.1 %. Net head 105.0–107.6 m. Gate 70.2–86.5 % except 1 min on the stop ramp (09:48). TGB shaft vibration X weekly means 55.5 / 55.5 / 55.3 / 56.6 µm pp (normal 35–70, CH §8.3). Headcover 1.0–1.3 mm/s. Thrust, UGB and LGB pad spreads at most 2.3, 1.9 and 1.8 K. Shear pins OK and no breakaway alarm for the whole period. Headcover sump pump starts were a constant 71 per day (no change in pit humidity).

**E5 – Oil condition, U1 TGB** (`oil-analysis_2021-2026.csv`). Sn 1.0 ppm (2021-02) → 3.6 (2024-05) → 5.4 (2025-02) → 8.1 (2025-08) → 10.9 (2026-02) → **14.2 (2026-08)**. TAN 0.11 → **0.38**. MPC 8 → **28**. All are in CH's Caution band, with tin and TAN just below Action (Sn > 15, TAN > 0.40; CH-DOC-OM-0101 §5.2). Oil in service since 2023-01-18 (WO-2023-0051), about 46 months by 11-03, against CH's 36-month maximum (§5.3). U2 and U3 TGB on 2026-08-09: Sn 3.1 / 3.3, TAN 0.22 / 0.21 (Normal). CH §5.2 says: "A rising tin trend together with rising TAN and MPC indicates oil oxidation and babbitt distress. Oxidised oil loses film strength." CH §4.2 adds that a segment tighter than 0.25 mm "runs hotter… and has less margin when the oil degrades".
