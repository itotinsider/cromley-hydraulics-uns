## 1 Problem statement

At the annual inspection on 20 October 2025, three cracks were found at stiffener welds of the Unit 2 draft tube cone, and cavitation erosion on the runner band. None was present at the 2024 inspection.

## 2 Impact

Repair outage 216 h after the inspection (WO-2025-0821, WO-2025-0834); approx. 7,400 MWh lost generation; repair cost approx. USD 185k. Risk avoided: a growing cone crack can lead to a draft tube liner failure and a long outage.

## 3 How it was detected

Visual inspection and NDT at the annual inspection. No vibration or pulsation monitoring existed on the units in 2025. Operators had noted "rough running" and noise on Unit 2 several times in July and August 2025 (shift log), without follow-up.

## 5 Evidence collected

Parts: crack replicas and photos, cavitation depth map. Position: crack locations in the cone zone where the vortex rope pressure pulsation acts. People: shift supervisors, System Ops, I&C. Paper: plant controller setpoint logs (used to estimate hours in the zone), MOC records, shift log, 2014 overhaul weld records, CH assessment by Lars Bergström. Paradigms: "the units have always run at part load sometimes, it's fine".

## 6 Hypotheses and verification

| # | Hypothesis | Evidence | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Fatigue from draft-tube vortex rope pulsation (part-load operation) | 640 h in the rough zone for Unit 2 vs. CH guidance of 50 h/year; crack location; CH assessment | **Verified** |
| H2 | Poor weld toe profile from the 2014 overhaul | Weld toe angle steep at the cracked stiffeners | Verified as **contributing** |
| H3 | Cavitation from low tailwater | Tailwater normal all summer | Ruled out |
| H4 | Cavitation from high load / high head | Unit 2 rarely above 90 % gate in the period | Ruled out |
| H5 | Cavitation from part-load operation | Erosion pattern at the band typical for part-load inter-blade vortices; coincides with H1 | **Verified** |

## 7 Root causes

| Type | Root cause | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical | Fatigue cracking of the cone stiffener welds and runner band cavitation from sustained operation in the vortex-rope zone (contributing: steep weld toes) | Inspection, hours estimate, CH assessment |
| Human | Bypass enabled and left in place beyond its expiry; Unit 2 used as the preferred regulating unit; "rough running" reports not escalated | MOC register, shift log |
| Latent | MOC process allowed a rough-zone bypass without reliability review against the OEM limit (CH-DOC-OM-0101 section 3) | MOC-2025-012 |
| Latent | Commercial value of the regulation reserve was weighed without the equipment cost; no agreed band outside the rough zone | Interview System Ops |
| Latent | No tracking of rough-zone hours and no vibration or pulsation monitoring | Design, KPIs |

## 9 Effectiveness check

Rough-zone hours per unit below 50 h per year (CH guidance), reviewed monthly from 2027 once A4 is in place. No new cone cracks at the 2026 and 2027 inspections.

## 10 Lessons and FMECA / PM updates

FMECA FM-07 added (vortex rope). Lesson: part-load operation is an operating decision with a maintenance cost; the signature (pulsation near 0.28 × runner frequency, high TGB vibration, MW swings) can now be seen in the CH monitoring data.
