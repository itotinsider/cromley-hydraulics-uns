## 1 Problem statement

On 19 August 2024 at 21:40, Unit 3 was stopped by the shift supervisor on a thrust bearing temperature alarm. The bearing was healthy; the measurement was wrong.

## 2 Impact

Unit unavailable for 14.2 h; approx. 510 MWh lost generation (approx. USD 31k). No damage, no safety effect. Linked work order WO-2024-0649.

## 3 How it was detected

Control room indication of thrust bearing pad 1 (the only thrust pad visible in the control room at that time) jumped between 66 and 84 °C. The 80 °C alarm was raised twice.

## 5 Evidence collected

Parts: corroded terminal (kept). Position: JB-U3-01 in the upper bracket, condensation marks inside the box. People: shift supervisor B, I&C on call. Paper: SCADA trend, alarm log, local oil thermometer on rounds sheet, 2017 overhaul wiring records. Paradigms: "if a bearing alarm comes, stop first, ask later".

## 6 Hypotheses and verification

| # | Hypothesis | Evidence | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Real thrust bearing overheating | Oil bath steady; temperature kept jumping after stop; bearing inspection normal | Ruled out |
| H2 | Cooling water problem | Total flow 80 L/s; other bearings normal | Ruled out |
| H3 | Measurement fault (RTD, cable, terminal) | Step changes too fast for a bearing (thermal time constant minutes); fault persisted with the unit stopped; loose corroded terminal found | **Verified** |

A bearing pad cannot change temperature by 18 K within a minute and back. Fast steps with a steady oil bath point to the measurement.

## 7 Root causes

| Type | Root cause | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical | Loose, corroded terminal in JB-U3-01 gave intermittent contact resistance on the pad 1 RTD | Terminal condition, loop resistance before/after |
| Human | Unit stopped without validating the alarm against other indications | Shift log, interview |
| Latent | No procedure for validating a bearing temperature alarm; operators had only one sensor per bearing | Procedures, design |
| Latent | No periodic inspection of junction boxes; condensation in the upper bracket box | PM plan, JB condition |

## 9 Effectiveness check

No spurious bearing-temperature stops since OP-07 (checked 2025-09-01). A4 still open.

## 10 Lessons and FMECA / PM updates

FMECA FM-06 added (bearing temperature measurement fault). Lesson: one channel moving fast and alone, with the oil bath steady, is a measurement problem until proven otherwise. Stopping a healthy unit is a failure too.
