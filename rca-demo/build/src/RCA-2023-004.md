## 1 Problem statement

On 14 March 2023 at 04:12, Unit 2 tripped on turbine guide bearing (TGB) temperature (pad 1, 90 °C). All four TGB pads were found lightly wiped.

## 2 Impact

Forced outage of 238 h (9.9 days), approx. 8,300 MWh lost generation. Repair: pad re-scraping by CH field service, oil change and flush, approx. USD 96k including lost revenue. No safety or environmental effect. Linked work order WO-2023-0204.

## 3 How it was detected

Automatic protection trip on the TGB pad 1 RTD. A total cooling water flow alarm (below 56 L/s) had been raised 52 minutes earlier and acknowledged without action.

## 5 Evidence collected

Parts: TGB pads (photographed, kept), strainer basket contents, oil sample before change. Position: debris in basket, wipe pattern on all four pads (uniform, worst at the loaded side). People: statements from the control room operator and shift supervisor C. Paper: SCADA trends (cooling flow, bearing temperatures), alarm log, rounds sheets, PM history, oil analysis Feb 2023 (normal). Paradigms: "the flow alarm always comes after rain, it's the meter".

## 6 Hypotheses and verification

| # | Hypothesis | Evidence | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Loss of cooling water to the TB circuit (strainer blocked) | Total flow 80 → 48 L/s; strainer DP 0.62 bar, silt-filled basket; all bearings warmed, TGB first | **Verified** |
| H2 | TGB oil degradation | Feb 2023 sample normal (TAN 0.17, Sn 2.6 ppm) | Ruled out |
| H3 | Operation in the rough zone | Gate 82 % at the time (outside 35–70 %) | Ruled out |
| H4 | TGB clearance out of tolerance | All segments measured within 0.30 ± 0.05 mm | Ruled out |
| H5 | Instrument fault | All pads wiped; oil temperature rose; other bearings warmed too | Ruled out |

Why the TGB failed first: the TGB has its own small cooling circuit (TB, 8 L/s nominal) and the least thermal margin; the generator bearings on the GB circuit warmed but stayed below alarm.

## 7 Root causes

| Type | Root cause | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical | Silt-blocked cooling water strainer reduced TB circuit flow; TGB oil overheated and the oil film broke down: pad wiping | Strainer DP, flow trend, wipe pattern, tin 31.5 ppm |
| Human | Flow alarm acknowledged without checking the strainer; strainer DP not read on night rounds | Alarm log, rounds sheet, interviews |
| Latent | Strainer cleaning on a fixed quarterly interval regardless of the silt season | PM plan |
| Latent | Flowmeter nuisance alarms had taught operators to ignore the flow alarm | Alarm history Jan–Mar 2023 |
| Latent | No circuit-level flow measurement and only one pad per bearing visible in the control room | Design (CH-DOC-DWG-0102) |

## 9 Effectiveness check

No cooling-related bearing alarms or trips on any unit in the 2024 and 2025 runoff seasons (checked 2024-04-30 and 2025-04-30). Effectiveness confirmed.

## 10 Lessons and FMECA / PM updates

FMECA FM-02 (strainer) occurrence and task updated. Lesson: a cooling problem shows on **all** bearings at once and on the cooling flow; the TGB is first to suffer because of its small circuit.
